# Google Play badges

Official digital PNG badges provided by Mauro:

- `google-play-en.png` is used when the site is in English.
- `google-play-es-419.png` is the Spanish Latin American version used when the site is in Spanish.
- `google-play-pt-br.png` is the Brazilian Portuguese version, saved for later.

The portfolio switches between the English and Spanish Latin American badges when the language changes. The Portuguese badge is saved but is not connected because the site does not currently offer Portuguese.

## Apple App Store badges

On pages with an App Store link, the badge uses Apple's official badge endpoint and switches between English (`en-us`) and Mexican Spanish (`es-mx`) with the portfolio language. Brazilian Portuguese (`pt-br`) is available from Apple's endpoint at:

`https://tools.applemediaservices.com/api/badges/download-on-the-app-store/black/pt-br?size=250x83`

The Portuguese badge is documented for later use and is not connected to the language toggle. Apple also provides downloadable localized badge artwork through its [official marketing tools](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/marketing/guidelines/).
