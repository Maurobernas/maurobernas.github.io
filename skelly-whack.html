Contributions

Grid and trap purchasing system
  • First version.
    • Developed a grid system over the terrain that allowed traps to be placed in available cells.
    • Implemented mechanics to select, move, reposition, and sell traps.
    • Developed trap purchasing and placement logic, including the ability to combine traps of the same type to increase their level.
  • Final version.
    • Adapted the system to a layout of invisible cells with predefined positions.
    • Implemented automatic placement of purchased traps in the first available cell.
    • Developed an individual progression system in which successive purchases raise a trap's level until it reaches its maximum, allowing another trap of the same type to be added.
    • Implemented mechanics to destroy traps.
Trap progression and visual representation
  • Developed the level-up logic.
  • Implemented visual changes based on each trap's level by updating its meshes and materials.
  • Configured purchase restrictions: the last trap must be at maximum level, and a cell must be available to place a new one.
Level progression and enemy spawning
  • Implemented progression mechanics based on defeating enemies to complete levels.
  • Adjusted progression to reset when a new level begins, requiring traps to be purchased and upgraded again.
  • Implemented an option to purchase faster enemy spawning, allowing players to advance more quickly.
