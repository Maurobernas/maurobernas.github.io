Rewards

Contributions

Package assessment and acceptance
  • Assessment of the package's condition and viability for the planned adaptation.
    • Identification of existing gameplay issues in the code.
    • Review of the number and quality of available levels.
  • Assessment of code changes needed to adapt gameplay and level structure.
Level flow
  • Google Sheets system for editing levels asynchronously.
    • Configuration of levels combining rounds and selected levels from the original package.
    • Configuration of objectives, time limits, attempts, and available power-ups.
    • Replacement of default words through the sheet in word-based games.
    • The Game Designer can add, remove, and modify levels online without needing a new APK build for testing.
Unity implementation
  • Bug fixes.
    • Gameplay.
    • Animations.
    • Code fixes for visual and audio effects that failed to display or play correctly.
  • Gameplay mechanic adjustments based on each game's needs.
  • Implementation of the Bitel power-up.
  • Implementation of time limits, lives, attempts, or other constraints according to the Game Designer's chosen design.
  • Implementation of tutorials.
  • Organization of reward-delivery stages within levels.
Art integration and optimization
  • Integration of visual assets provided by the art team.
  • Integration of the company mascot, including animations, dialogue, and sounds.
  • Asset compression to reduce APK size.
